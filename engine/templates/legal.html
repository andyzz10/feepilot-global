Bringing Radical Transparency to Payment Processing Fees

Digital businesses, SaaS founders, e-commerce sellers, and global freelancers lose billions of dollars every year to hidden payment processing fees, cross-border FX markups, and fixed micro-transaction surcharges.

METHODOLOGY

How Our 50+ Calculators Work

Each platform's rate comes from its published pricing. Rates that were checked against an official source carry a "checked" label with the month; all other rates are shown as estimates and should be confirmed on the platform's own site before you rely on them. Where a platform has several plans or regions (for example Square Free vs Plus, or Stripe in the US, UK and EEA), you can pick the one that applies to you.

  • 🌍 Plans, Regions & Surcharges: Rates for the plans and regions we have checked can be selected individually. International-card and currency-conversion surcharges are added separately, because platforms charge them separately. Anything else can be modelled with the custom-rate option.
  • ⚡ Reverse Fee Formula: Calculates the exact gross amount you must invoice a client (`(Amount + Fixed Fee) ÷ (1 − Fee %)`) so that you receive 100% of your target net payout without absorbing the fee.
